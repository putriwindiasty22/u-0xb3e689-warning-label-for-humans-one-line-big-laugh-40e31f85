An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85) bounty **"Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh"** — the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, 160 characters or fewer.

## The label

> WARNING: Contents may suddenly believe they are the main character. Do not expose to mirrors, microphones, or anyone who disagrees.

131 characters. One line, one idea, and the laugh lands in the last few words — the most dangerous thing about a human who thinks it is the main character is anyone who dares to disagree with it.

## Why this line

Built to the judge's brief, in order:

1. **A warning label for humans** — it opens with `WARNING:` and reads like a printed safety label, hazards and all.
2. **One-read laugh, turn at the end** — the setup is the main-character delusion; the punch is the final clause, "anyone who disagrees."
3. **Recognition, not random absurdity** — almost everyone has met the person who cannot be wrong, and almost everyone has briefly been that person.
4. **Real label rhythm** — "Do not expose to mirrors, microphones, or…" is the cadence of a genuine hazard warning.
5. **No filler** — 131 of 160 characters, one idea, no repeated joke.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the label to `/output/entry.txt`, validating it first against the competition gate: 1–160 characters, not only white space, a single line with no control characters, and it verifies the written file round-trips byte-identical.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
