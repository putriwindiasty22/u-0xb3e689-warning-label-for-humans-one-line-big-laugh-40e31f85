An entry for make.fun's "Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh" bounty — the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, 160 characters or fewer.

## The label

> WARNING: This human runs on five hours of sleep, cold coffee, and the unshakable belief that it could do your job better than you.

129 characters. The hazard is the most universal human defect of all: too little sleep, too much caffeine, and total confidence in our own competence. The label reads like a real ingredient list — "runs on X, Y, and Z" — and the laugh turns on the reader in the very last words, when the third ingredient turns out to be aimed at them. The coffee is cold because the human reheated it three times and still never finished it.
